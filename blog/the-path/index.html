The Path

Sanjay Das · Poetry

Walking on a path no one took,
Mile after mile, I went on
To get somewhere
I feel I belong,
Where I can rest
with a tiresome smile
on a proud face
with my head held high…

Still, nothing in sight
except the mysterious path
engulfed in fog,
full of walking shadows
getting thicker and thicker
as far as the eye goes…

With trembling legs and
a fearful heart
full of hopes and dreams,
alone, all alone, I went on…
