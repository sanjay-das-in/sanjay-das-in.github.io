Research · Oak Ridge National Laboratory

Hi, I’m Sanjay.

I work on making AI useful for science—and reliable enough for the decisions around it.

I’m a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Oak Ridge National Laboratory. My work brings together scientific discovery, agentic AI, and operational safety.

Some of the questions I study: Can AI help us evaluate a scientific hypothesis? Can a conversation between agents improve hazard analysis? What happens when the hardware behind a model goes wrong?

My background is in AI and hardware reliability, with a Ph.D. from UT Dallas. Outside the lab, I enjoy photography and sharing what I learn.

Research directions

Research

Connecting scientific reasoning, operational safety, and the systems underneath AI.

01 / AI for science

Scientific discovery & hypothesis generation

Developing structured, reproducible workflows that help researchers generate and evaluate testable hypotheses. Current questions include knowledge-graph context, intrinsic model scoring, and scientific reasoning.

Knowledge graphsScientific reasoning
Graph context ↗Hypothesis evaluation ↗
02 / AI for operations

Agentic AI for operational safety

Studying dialogue, retrieved evidence, and human feedback for hazard identification and risk analysis. This includes HAZDIAL, HARNESS, and human-guided high-risk property classification with ORCHID.

Agentic AIHuman oversight
HAZDIAL ↗HARNESS ↗ORCHID ↗
03 / Reliable AI systems

Model reliability & hardware safety

Investigating adversarial bit flips, hardware-induced faults, and fault mitigation. The work connects model behavior with its computing substrate, from binary networks and LLMs to compute-in-memory accelerators.
